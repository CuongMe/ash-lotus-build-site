Ash Lotus is a personal, curated modlist designed to enhance the vanilla Fallout 4 experience—not replace it. It expands the world with high-quality quest and adventure mods like America Rising 2, Depravity, and Project Valkyrie, while preserving the original tone of the game.

🔫 Only one modern weapon (RU556) is added as a bonus, ensuring minimal disruption to the vanilla atmosphere. 🧥 Most vanilla weapons, armor, and items are retextured in 2K for better visuals, with a few carefully selected lore-friendly additions. 🎨 Includes a custom ReShade featuring subtle HDR effects, delivering an immersive and cinematic experience without over-saturation or ENB.

Whether you're diving back into the Commonwealth or exploring new storylines, Ash Lotus aims to bring depth, style, and stability.
